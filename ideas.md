# Design Ideas for Solana Token Launcher

## Approach 1 — Cosmic Brutalism
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalism meets Cosmic Dark

**Core Principles:**
- Raw, heavy typography with stark contrast against near-black backgrounds
- Asymmetric layouts with intentional "broken grid" feel
- Neon accent colors (electric violet + acid green) as hard borders and glows
- Functional, no-nonsense form design with brutalist card borders

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: #070710 (near-black with blue undertone)
- Primary accent: #7C3AED (electric violet)
- Secondary accent: #22D3EE (cyan)
- Text: #F8FAFC (near-white)
- Cards: #0F0F1A with 1px solid #7C3AED border

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero with Earth-from-space background
- Off-center headline text (left-aligned, massive)
- Brutalist card borders (hard shadows, no blur)
- Staggered feature cards in asymmetric grid

**Signature Elements:**
- Hard-edged neon borders on cards
- Oversized stat numbers with heavy weight
- Pill-shaped nav with solid border

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Buttons have hard translate-y hover effects (no blur)
- Cards lift with hard box-shadow offset
- Form inputs glow on focus

**Animation:**
- Entrance: fade-up with stagger
- Hover: hard 2px translate
- CTA: pulsing border glow

**Typography System:**
- Display: Space Grotesk Bold (700) for headlines
- Body: DM Sans (400/500) for readability
- Mono: JetBrains Mono for addresses/hashes
</text>

## Approach 2 — Glassmorphic Space Station
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Glassmorphism + Deep Space Aesthetic

**Core Principles:**
- Frosted glass panels floating over deep space backgrounds
- Soft purple-to-blue gradient universe as the canvas
- Layered depth through blur, transparency, and soft glows
- Smooth, fluid animations that feel weightless

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep space gradient from #03001C to #1A0533
- Glass cards: rgba(255,255,255,0.05) with backdrop-blur
- Primary: #9333EA (vivid purple)
- Glow: #A855F7 with 0 0 40px rgba(168,85,247,0.4)
- Text: Pure white with varying opacity

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-screen hero with animated star field
- Centered content with glass card overlays
- Floating feature cards with glass effect
- Gradient mesh background sections

**Signature Elements:**
- Glass morphism cards with subtle border glow
- Animated particle/star background
- Gradient text (purple to cyan)

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover reveals deeper glass layers
- Smooth 300ms transitions everywhere
- Wallet connect triggers ripple effect

**Animation:**
- Star field parallax on scroll
- Cards float in on viewport entry
- Gradient text shimmer

**Typography System:**
- Display: Outfit ExtraBold (800) for headlines
- Body: Outfit Regular (400)
- Accent: Syne for special labels
</text>

## Approach 3 — Dark Solana Native (CHOSEN)
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Dark Tech / Crypto Native — inspired by Solana's own design language

**Core Principles:**
- Pure black backgrounds with subtle purple/violet gradients
- Clean, modern typography with strong hierarchy
- Rounded pill nav, gradient CTAs, card-based layouts
- Professional yet energetic — feels like a real DeFi product

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: #000000 to #0A0A14 (pure black with subtle depth)
- Primary: #9945FF (Solana purple)
- Secondary: #14F195 (Solana green)
- Accent gradient: linear from #9945FF to #14F195
- Cards: #111118 with subtle border rgba(153,69,255,0.2)
- Text: #FFFFFF primary, #94A3B8 secondary

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero with Earth-from-space imagery
- Left-aligned headline with moon emoji accent
- Centered token creation form in a dark card
- Feature cards in a 3-column grid
- Statistics displayed as large bold numbers

**Signature Elements:**
- Gradient text (purple to green, Solana brand)
- Pill-shaped navigation with glass border
- Glowing CTA buttons with gradient fill

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth hover glow on buttons
- Form inputs with purple focus ring
- Wallet connection with animated state change

**Animation:**
- Smooth fade-up on scroll entrance
- Button hover: scale(1.02) + glow intensify
- Wallet connect: loading spinner then address display

**Typography System:**
- Display: Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold (800) for headlines
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans Regular (400/500)
- Code/addresses: font-mono for wallet addresses
</text>

## Selected Approach: Dark Solana Native (Approach 3)

This approach mirrors the professional DeFi aesthetic of luna-coin.com while establishing a distinct identity. The Solana brand colors (purple #9945FF and green #14F195) create immediate recognition for crypto-native users, while the clean dark background ensures the token creation form is the focal point.
